About

Building strong hooves from the ground up.

My focus goes less to the angles and more to the health and strength of the bare hoof. I believe that should be optimized before considering a prosthetic, unless it's actually going to improve the health and strength of the foot.

Frequent proper trimming to simulate natural wear and promote healthy development works by knowing the shapes of the internal anatomy and finding the natural shapes in the external anatomy. There are more curves than straight lines inside and out — these curves need to be respected and built over time to achieve optimal alignment and integrity.

True strength comes from building the core of the digital cushion, not from propping a weak foot up on a false external foundation.
