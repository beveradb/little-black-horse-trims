What We Do

Services

Hooves of all sizes welcome — donkeys, minis, drafts, and everything in between.

Barefoot Trimming

Natural hoof care focused on building healthy, strong bare hooves through frequent proper trimming that simulates natural wear.

Hoof Building

Corrective and rehabilitative trimming to restore your horse's true angles and develop a strong, well-balanced hoof over time.

Glue-Ons

Composite shoes for horses needing extra support during rehabilitation or for comfort and soundness.

Philosophy

About

True strength comes from building the core of the digital cushion, not from propping a weak foot up on a false external foundation.

My focus goes less to the angles and more to the health and strength of the bare hoof. I believe that should be optimized before considering a prosthetic, unless it's actually going to improve the health and strength of the foot.

Frequent proper trimming to simulate natural wear and promote healthy development works by knowing the shapes of the internal anatomy and finding the natural shapes in the external anatomy. There are more curves than straight lines inside and out — these curves need to be respected and built over time.
